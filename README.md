# Buy or Invest?

A simple tool to visualize the opportunity cost of purchases by showing what that money could become if invested instead.

## Features

- **Opportunity cost calculator** — see what your purchase could grow to by retirement
- **Retirement impact** — shows how many days of your target retirement budget you're spending
- **Portfolio percentage** — visualizes what % of your retirement goal this represents
- **Fully configurable** — DOB, retirement age, expected return, target portfolio, withdrawal rate
- **Auto-save** — all settings persist in localStorage
- **Investment-agnostic** — works with any investment vehicle (ETFs, stocks, index funds, etc.)
- **Currency-agnostic** — works with any currency

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Enter the amount you're considering spending
3. Configure your settings (DOB, retirement age, expected return, etc.)
4. See the long-term impact of your purchase decision

## Settings

All settings are saved automatically in localStorage:

- **Date of Birth** — used to calculate years until retirement
- **Retirement Age** — target retirement age
- **Expected Annual Return (%)** — expected annual return on investment
- **Target Retirement Portfolio** — your FIRE number / retirement goal
- **Withdrawal Rate (%)** — safe withdrawal rate for retirement income
